Calibrate a camera

Click the near and far ends of dashed lane-line segments on the empty-road frame. For each point, say which lane line it is on (0, 1, …, counted across the road), which dash along that line (0 is the nearest), and which end. Dashes are 10 ft with 30 ft gaps, and lanes are 12 ft wide.

Load the saved points or click at least 4 points, then fit.

Save the download as cctv/data/calibration/<camera>.points.json, then run python -m cctv.calibrate fit --camera <camera> to write the calibration the pipeline uses. Nothing is saved by this page.

Speeds are estimates with stated uncertainty, for traffic analytics only. They are not evidence of any violation, and no vehicle or person is identified.
